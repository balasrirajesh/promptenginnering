    💡 Prompt Engineering Takeaways
    01. Resolve Contradictions

    Identify conflicting instructions and establish priorities. If two requirements cannot both be satisfied, explain the trade-off.

    02. Remove Ambiguity

    Replace unclear pronouns and vague expressions with explicit subjects, actions, and expected outcomes.

    03. Define Output Formats

    Specify the required structure, fields, length, and formatting rules to make results easier to evaluate.

    04. Supply Missing Context

    Provide relevant information, examples, and constraints. Ask clarifying questions when essential details are unavailable.
