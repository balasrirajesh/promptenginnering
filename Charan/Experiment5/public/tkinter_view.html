● Engine: gemini-2.5-flash | Light Theme Ready
Aim: Execute a zero-shot prompt vs. a few-shot prompt (with 2 exemplar pairs); compare output formatting, consistency, and structural adherence.
Input Query / Concept:
Explain inertia. ▾

PART A: ZERO-SHOT PROMPTING

No Exemplars
Inertia is the inherent physical property of matter that causes an object to resist any change in its existing state of motion or rest. Unless acted upon by an external net force, an object at rest will remain at rest, and an object moving at a constant velocity will continue to move in a straight line at that same speed.
Latency: 0.84s | Words: 49 | Format: Brief, unguided direct response

PART B: FEW-SHOT PROMPTING

2 Exemplar Pairs
Inertia is the fundamental tendency of any physical body to resist alterations to its state of motion or equilibrium, directly quantified by its mass. Examples: 1. A seated car passenger jolts forward when the driver suddenly stamps on the brakes, because the passenger's body continues forward due to inertia. 2. A heavy hockey puck glides smoothly across frictionless ice in a straight line without slowing down until intercepted by a stick or barrier.
Latency: 1.12s | Words: 73 | Schema Adherence: 100% PASS (Numbered Exemplars 1. & 2. Produced)
COMPARATIVE EVALUATION & EMPIRICAL FINDINGS
• Query Evaluated: "Explain inertia."
• Format Adherence: [PASSED] Few-shot successfully reproduced the 2-part schema (Conceptual Explanation + Numbered Examples 1 & 2) demonstrated in the exemplars.
• Output Consistency: Zero-shot produced 49 words (0.84s) as a raw summary; Few-shot produced 73 words (1.12s) with concrete, real-world anchored exemplars.
• Conclusion: In-context demonstration exemplars steer generative LLMs into standardized structure and reduced cognitive variance without model fine-tuning.
